About us

A Lahore firm for journeys that leave Lahore.

Our story

Immigration advice without the theatrics.

Links Immigration Consultants works from Big City Plaza in Gulberg, in the middle of Lahore's business district. Our consultants bring more than twenty years of combined experience across student, visit, work, family, skilled and business visa categories for Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom and the United States.

The industry we work in has a reputation problem: too many agents promise guaranteed visas and disappear after the fee. We built this firm on the opposite habit. The first consultation is free, the assessment is honest, and if your profile does not qualify for a route, we say so before you spend money on it.

When you become a client, one case manager owns your file. You know what has been submitted, what is pending, and what the realistic timeline looks like.
