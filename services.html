Visa services

Pick the route. We handle the paperwork.

Study

Student visas

University and college admissions guidance, offer letters, financial documentation, statements of purpose and study-permit filing for Canada, Australia, the UK, the USA and New Zealand. We also advise on post-study work options so your program choice supports your longer plan.

Visit

Visit & tourist visas

Visitor applications built on complete, honest documentation: travel history, invitations, sponsorship letters and ties to Pakistan, presented the way visa officers expect to read them. We also handle visit visas for Schengen countries, the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

Work

Work permits & LMIA

Employer-driven work permits, including Canadian Labour Market Impact Assessment support. We coordinate with employers on the paperwork their side requires as well as yours.

Family

Family, spouse & partner visas

Spouse, partner, parent and dependent sponsorships. We prepare relationship evidence and financial documentation so families are not kept apart by an avoidable refusal.

Migrate

Skilled immigration

Points-based permanent residence pathways: Canada's Express Entry and provincial nominee programs, and the skilled-migration streams of Australia and New Zealand. We assess your score honestly before you spend on tests and assessments.

Invest

Business & investor visas

Entrepreneur and investor routes for business owners looking to expand abroad, with business-plan and source-of-funds documentation support.

Refused?

Appeals & judicial reviews

A refusal is not always the end. We obtain and study the officer's notes, identify what went wrong, and prepare appeals, reconsiderations or judicial reviews where the case supports one. Bring your refusal letter to a free consultation and we will give you a straight answer on whether an appeal is worth it.

Common questions

Before you ask, others asked too.

Are you licensed?

Yes — your file is handled through regulated practitioners. UK matters are filed under IAA registration F202100269 (Prime Law Chambers, Surbiton) and Canadian matters with RCIC License R406943 (Ali Anwar, Surrey BC). Both can be checked on the official government registers, linked on our About page.

How much does the first consultation cost?

Nothing. Your first assessment is free, in person at our Gulberg office or over WhatsApp.

Which countries do you handle?

Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom and the United States, across student, visit, work, family, skilled and business categories.

Can you guarantee a visa?

No honest consultant can. Decisions rest with the visa officer. What we control is the quality and completeness of your application, and that is where refusals are usually won or lost.

I was refused before. Can I apply again?

Absolutely yes. Once the refusal reasons are properly addressed, your new application can succeed. Bring your refusal letter and we will review it with you.
